# Remote.Me Purchases and Subscriptions

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Remote.Me Free is available without a paid entitlement. Pro is offered as a
one-time purchase and Enterprise as an auto-renewable annual subscription where
available. Current price, trial, tax, territory, and renewal information is shown by
the App Store purchase sheet and takes precedence over documentation or screenshots.

## Purchase processing

Apple processes payment and subscription management. RevenueCat is used to retrieve
available products and validate Pro and Enterprise entitlements. Remote.Me does not
receive payment-card details.

## Restore access

1. Sign in to the Mac App Store with the Apple Account used for purchase.
2. Open Remote.Me and select **Restore Purchases**.
3. Allow the entitlement refresh to finish.
4. Confirm the app displays **Remote.Me Pro** or **Remote.Me Enterprise**.

An Enterprise subscription must remain active to unlock Enterprise settings. If it
expires, saved organization settings may remain visible but read-only. Apple manages
cancellation and renewal in the account's subscription settings.

For purchase problems, use [Support and feedback](support.md) and omit receipts,
full transaction identifiers, Apple Account details, and payment information.
