# Remote.Me Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Remote.Me is developed by Morten Buus.

## Remote Connections

Remote.Me is designed to establish direct connections between Macs. Remote
screen images and keyboard and mouse input are transmitted only as needed for
the remote session. The developer does not operate a Remote.Me service that
stores the contents of remote sessions.

Only accept a connection when you recognize and trust the connecting device.

## Local Network And Bonjour

Remote.Me requests local-network access to discover and connect to nearby Macs.
Bonjour advertisements contain technical discovery information such as the
device name, app and protocol compatibility, availability, and supported
capabilities.

Other devices on the same local network may be able to see this discovery
information while Remote.Me is advertising its availability.

## Data Stored On Your Mac

Remote.Me may store app preferences, permission status, paired-device
information, recently connected machines, connection history, organization
settings, and purchase-entitlement status locally on the Mac.

Connection history can include a machine name, local IP address, port,
connection time, result, and duration. Remote screen contents, passwords, and
payment-card information are not stored in the connection history.

## Purchases

Apple processes App Store purchases and payment information. RevenueCat is used
to retrieve products, process and restore purchases through Apple, and determine
whether Remote.Me Pro or Remote.Me Enterprise features are available.

Apple and RevenueCat may process transaction identifiers, product identifiers,
subscription status, app-user identifiers, device or app information, and
related diagnostic information under their respective privacy policies:

- [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/)

The developer does not receive or store payment-card details.

## Diagnostics And Support

Remote.Me displays connection and network diagnostics to help troubleshoot
local-network problems. Users choose whether to include any of this information
in a support request.

Support requests are submitted through the public
[Remote.Me support tracker](https://github.com/buusmorten/documentation/issues/new?template=support.yml).
Information submitted there is processed by GitHub and may be publicly visible.

Do not submit passwords, pairing codes, payment information, private keys,
private screen contents, full public IP addresses, or other credentials.

## Advertising And Tracking

Remote.Me does not display third-party advertising and is not designed to track
users across apps or websites for advertising purposes.
