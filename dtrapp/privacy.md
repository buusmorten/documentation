# DTRAPP / Den Tunge Radio Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 31, 2026

Den Tunge Radio is developed by Morten Buus. The app does not require a Den Tunge
Radio account and does not use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

## Information handled by the app

The app stores preferences, selected program reminders, saved tracks, and private
listening statistics on the device. Saved tracks and listening statistics may
synchronize through the user's private Apple iCloud account when iCloud is available.

The app can send an anonymous saved-track event to the Den Tunge Radio Supabase
backend for aggregate station statistics. The event can include track title, artist,
album, artwork and music-service links, save time, app version, platform, and source.
It is designed not to include a user ID, device ID, advertising identifier, email,
Apple ID, account profile, or location.

## Online services

The app connects to services required for functionality, including:

- SAM Cloud / Spacial for radio streaming and station metadata;
- Den Tunge Radio web services for program information and station links;
- Mixcloud when the user opens podcast content;
- Apple services for iCloud, widgets, Live Activities, notifications, and system
  media controls; and
- Supabase for anonymous aggregate saved-track events.

Network providers and these services receive ordinary connection information such as
IP address, request time, and device/network metadata under their own policies.

## Notifications and background audio

Program reminders are scheduled using Apple notification services after permission is
granted. Background audio allows the live stream to continue while the app is not in
the foreground or the device is locked.

## Analytics, advertising, and sale of data

The app does not use an advertising identifier, advertising SDK, or cross-app tracking
SDK. Den Tunge Radio does not sell personal information collected through the app.
Anonymous aggregate saved-track events are used to understand which music listeners
choose to save; they are not intended to identify individual listeners.

## Retention and choices

Local data remains until removed in the app or with the app's device data. iCloud
copies are controlled through the user's Apple account and backups. Anonymous backend
events may be retained for aggregate station statistics. Users can decline notification
permission and can use local saved tracks when iCloud is unavailable.

## Children and changes

The app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.
This policy may be updated when app functionality or service providers change.
