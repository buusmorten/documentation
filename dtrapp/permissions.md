# DTRAPP Permissions and Device Features

Last updated: July 31, 2026

- **Notifications** are optional and used for reminders before selected programs.
- **Background audio** keeps the selected live radio stream playing when the app is
  backgrounded or the device is locked.
- **iCloud** can synchronize saved tracks and private listening statistics across the
  user's devices and help restore them after reinstalling.
- **Widgets and Live Activities** display current station or Now Playing information
  on supported Apple system surfaces.

The user can review notification permission in iOS/iPadOS Settings and control iCloud
access through Apple Account and iCloud settings. Disabling a permission or service
removes or limits the corresponding feature; live listening and local behavior may
continue where technically possible.
